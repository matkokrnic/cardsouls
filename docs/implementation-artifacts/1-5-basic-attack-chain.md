# Story 1.5: Basic attack chain

Status: ready-for-dev

## Story

As a player,
I want the basic attack's hitbox lifetime, per-swing dedupe, and damage to live in state, with landed hits generating mana directly behind the melee-mana flag,
so that the attack is deterministic, replay-safe, and the P2 flywheel starts in E1 as data-tunable behavior rather than waiting on E3.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Chain — 1-3 reference, not scope (B5).** Chain transition logic is 1-3's landed, test-pinned behavior — `chain_index` advance/cap/reset, chain-window gating, `INPUT_PRIORITY`, the silent capped-chain reject, one transition per tick (`match_state.gd` `_try_transition`; `test_action_state.gd` chain pins; decision-log Session 2026-07-23 — Story 1-3 close-out). 1-5 neither re-specifies nor modifies any of it. No links, juggles, or cancels remains the design (GDD §F) and is already enforced by the 1-3 table.
2. **Hitbox is state data — DERIVED, not stored (N3).** `HeroState` exposes `is_hitbox_active` as a **derived accessor**: true iff the attack `active` window is running (which only happens during `ATTACKING`) — the 1-3 "phases are derived, not stored" contract (decision-log Session 2026-07-23), same family as `attack_phase()`. **No stored flag field**; the chain index for per-swing tuning already exists and is snapshotted. This story's snapshot delta is the per-swing dedupe tracking ONLY (AC 7). Actor-side consumption of the accessor is 1-7 scope — no actor work in 1-5.
3. **Mana on hit — direct implementation, ONE generation path, flag-gated (B1/B2, DEBT D resolved).** Basic attack costs no resource (GDD `gdd.md:139`/`:143`; 1-4 gate record "Basic attack stays FREE"). On a **confirmed hit** (a contact fact that survived AC 7's dedupe/liveness rules), mana accrues through **exactly ONE generation path** — the analog of 1-4's single deduction path — with the per-hit amount read **inline** as `ms.balance.melee_hit_mana` at the moment the hit is confirmed (CONSTRAINT C — never cached; the `attack_chain_length`/`roll_stamina_cost` pattern), gated on the **INJECTED** `FeatureFlags.melee_mana_generation` (default ON for E1, `feature_flags.gd:15`). This is the DEBT D resolution at its committed trigger: **no `EconomyEvaluator`, no `ResourceGenerationRule`, no `src/state/economy/`** — evaluator extraction is deferred with **E3 as the committed trigger** (decision-log Session 2026-07-25 — Story 1-5 readiness gate). `ManaPool.add()` exists and the pool starts empty by flywheel design (`player_state.gd:25`).
4. **Flag injection seam (B3) — 1-5 builds the first flag consumer in state.** The runner reads `FeatureFlagsService` **ONCE at match start** and injects the `FeatureFlags` resource into `MatchState`; state code never reads the service (HARD RULE, `project-context.md:134`, `feature_flags.gd:9-10`). Flags are load-once — **no reload path, deliberately unlike balance** (`feature_flags_service.gd:7`). Rule (a): the injected flags are **EXCLUDED from `to_snapshot()`** — flags are config, not state (analog of the 1-2 camera-basis exclusion, `match_state.gd:33-37`), so the golden hash never depends on the flag object.
5. **Contact intake seam (B7) — specified here so 1-7 consumes the SAME seam.** Contact facts enter `MatchState` as **queued contact events, drained deterministically inside `advance()` step 4** (game-architecture.md:253, :275-278), payload matching E1.S7 item 2: **attacker slot, target slot, attack index**. Step 4 resolves contacts — damage (AC 6) + dedupe (AC 7) — and **records confirmed hits**; step 5 applies the single mana-generation path from them (the `_regen_stamina` policy-seat analog), keeping the documented D2 order truthful (N4). 1-5's headless tests feed **synthetic** facts through this API; real runner-gathered facts arrive in 1-7 and MUST use this seam, not a second path. Rule (a) — X5 replay integrity: contact facts mutate state (damage, mana), so the intake shape must be **RECORDABLE alongside intents** in the replay stream — 1-5 shapes the API as plain recordable data; actual recording may land with 1-7 when the real runner feed exists.
6. **Damage formula pinned (N1).** damage = `attack_damage_percent_of_max_hp / 100.0` × **target's** max HP, read inline at resolution. Applying damage queues the existing `hp_changed` — **NO `hit_landed` signal in 1-5**; it stays in 1-7 with its presentation consumer (N5). Tuning note: the authored `10.0` sits above the GDD's ~5–8% chip band (`gdd.md:127`, `:138`); lower it toward the band (suggest `6.0`) during the dev pass — a data change, not story scope.
7. **Per-swing dedupe + stale-contact rule (B7b/N2, pinned).** One swing damages a given target at most once, tracked in state and **snapshotted** — mid-swing dedupe state excluded from the snapshot would be a determinism/replay hole (D8, the exact argument that snapshotted the 1-4 regen-delay window). Lifetime rule: the per-swing dedupe record lives **exactly ONE tick past active-window close**, absorbing the F1 one-tick fact lag. Acceptance rule: an incoming fact is accepted **iff its attack index matches a swing whose dedupe record is still alive**, otherwise dropped. A contact legitimately gathered on the last active tick is therefore accepted; anything older is dropped. Both sides of that boundary are headless-tested (AC 10).
8. **Movement during attack (B6 — operator decision, locked).** New balance field `attack_move_speed_multiplier` (`BalanceConfig` scalar, NOT tick-domain), applied in `_resolve_movement` while `action_state == ATTACKING`, **uniform across windup/active/recovery**, read inline at the moment of use. First authored value: **`0.0` — full root**. The multiplier scales the resolved **velocity only**; the facing update is untouched (facing remains raw intent-space and is consumed by nothing in 1-5 — gate fact N6). Per-phase multipliers are explicitly out of scope until animations exist. This resolves the **ATTACK half** of the 1-3 deferred action×movement coupling (`1-3-hero-action-state-machine.md:46`, `match_state.gd:75`); the **ROLL half remains assigned to 1-9** — the deferral is re-homed, not orphaned.
9. **Balance schema (B4).** Exactly TWO new `BalanceConfig` fields: `melee_hit_mana` (per-event amount, AC 3) and `attack_move_speed_multiplier` (scalar, AC 8) — **neither on `BalanceTicks`** (no tick-domain derivation exists for either). **NO `max_mana`** — the mana cap stays the runner's constructor value until story 3-1, as the runner comment records (`match_runner.gd:43`). Both fields are authored in `data/balance/balance_config.tres`, covered by the `.tres` smoke test (present, non-negative) and by `test_balance_authoring.gd`: `melee_hit_mana > 0` (a zero faucet is a dead flywheel — the flag is the off-switch, not a zero amount); `attack_move_speed_multiplier` **EXEMPT from > 0** with a one-line header reason (`0.0` = full root IS the authored design value), asserted non-negative only.
10. **Headless tests, including the flag matrix (B3b).** `is_hitbox_active` is true for exactly the active-window ticks; the same target contacted twice in one swing takes damage once; mana accrues once per confirmed hit; the stale-contact boundary both sides (a fact from the last active tick, arriving one tick later, is accepted; a fact whose dedupe record has expired is dropped); flag matrix — `melee_mana_generation` **ON** → mana accrues per confirmed hit, **OFF** → the hit lands and damage applies but mana stays zero; movement root — velocity is scaled by the multiplier across all three attack phases and restored on exit from `ATTACKING`.
11. **Golden discipline (B8).** ONE re-baseline, separately named causes, executed **only after implementation is complete and all non-golden tests are proven green** (the 65811b3 / 1-4 pattern). Expected causes, named separately in the commit message and Dev Agent Record: **(a)** snapshot shape — per-swing dedupe tracking enters `to_snapshot()` (D8); **(b)** `_golden_config()` authors real `attack_damage_percent_of_max_hp` and `melee_hit_mana`, and the recorded sequence feeds a synthetic confirmed hit; **(c)** if the recorded sequence holds movement input during a swing, movement-root (AC 8) — the current sequence's `MOVES` cycle is nonzero on swing ticks, so expect (c) to fire; verify and name it. The scenario must end with **non-full HP and non-zero mana on record** — a hitless scenario is blind to the entire combat layer. Add a pinning test analogous to `test_golden_sequence_exercises_stamina_spend_and_regen` (exact damage and mana arithmetic at named ticks) so the sequence can never silently degrade back to a hitless run. Golden-path flags are constructed **in-test** with `melee_mana_generation` ON — never read from the authored `.tres` (the same tuning-cannot-move-the-hash principle as `_golden_config`).

**OUT OF SCOPE (DEBT D + fences):** no `EconomyEvaluator`, `ResourceGenerationRule`, or `src/state/economy/` (evaluator extraction deferred; E3 is the committed trigger); no `max_mana` field (3-1); no `Area3D`/actor/runner contact gathering (1-7); no `hit_landed` signal (1-7); no per-phase movement multipliers (post-animations); no roll×movement coupling (1-9); no flag reload path (flags are load-once by design); no HUD/readout (E2); no chain re-specification (1-3).

## Dev Notes

- **Why hitbox lifetime and dedupe live in state** (not an enabled/disabled `Area3D`): headless-testable and replay-safe. The actual `Area3D` overlap wiring arrives in 1-7 — actors REPORT, state DECIDES. [Source: stories-manual-e1.md#E1.S5 items 2,4; docs/game-architecture.md#Spatial Model]
- **DEBT D resolved at its committed trigger (this story):** mana accrual is implemented DIRECTLY, same pattern as 1-4 stamina — one generation path, flag-gated, inline balance read. A rule schema extracted from two direct implementations (1-4 stamina, 1-5 mana) will be shaped by real cases when E3 lands the full D6 form. The evaluator references in `stories-manual-e1.md#E1.S5` item 3 are superseded by this resolution — decision-log Session 2026-07-25 is authoritative. Story 3-4's evaluator references reconcile at its own gate.
- **Mana pulled forward from E3, deliberately:** the GDD epic table (`gdd.md:411`) places melee-hit mana under E3; landing the accrual now, flag-gated with the flag default ON, is the recorded DEBT D resolution — not an oversight, and not a GDD edit.
- **Flags are config, not state:** injected once, no reload path (deliberately unlike balance — `feature_flags_service.gd:7`), excluded from the snapshot exactly as the 1-2 camera basis is. The flag-off path is the graceful degradation (project-context HARD RULE); with `melee_mana_generation` OFF the attack still lands and damages — only the faucet closes.
- **B6 rationale (movement root):** attack commitment is the core of the soulsborne half — a swing with no movement risk has no whiff-punish, and P2 prices aggression. The knob-not-code shape (`attack_move_speed_multiplier`) lets playtest move between rooted / slowed / free purely in data. Uniform across phases until animations exist.
- **Stale-contact rule rationale (N2):** runner-pushed facts lag one tick (F1; 1-7 asserts the constant lag). A dedupe record living exactly one tick past active-window close makes the last-active-tick contact legitimate and everything older droppable — a deliberate, tested rule instead of an accident 1-7 would inherit.
- **D2 order stays truthful (N4):** step 4 = contacts (damage + dedupe + confirmed-hit recording); step 5 = resource generation (mana from confirmed hits, alongside 1-4's stamina regen). Mana is not added inside step 4.
- **No combo system** — chains, not links. [Source: gdd.md#F Real-Time Combat — No traditional combo system]
- **`max_mana` exclusion:** the cap stays the runner's constructor placeholder (`_MAX_MANA`, `match_runner.gd:17`, comment at `:43`) until 3-1 folds the constants into the config object; adding it here would drag in the reload-refill question D9 answered for stamina only.
